# Into Bhutan

An interactive, single-page journey that makes the case for visiting Bhutan. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the repo with GitHub Pages.

The page descends from orbit into the kingdom:

1. **Earth**: a scroll-driven globe that turns and zooms to Bhutan (27.5° N, 90.4° E).
2. **Seven Wonders**: a framed cinematic slider: Tiger's Nest, Punakha Dzong, Gangkhar Puensum, Dochula Pass, Buddha Dordenma, Phobjikha Valley and Jomolhari.
3. **Why go**: forest cover, carbon-negative status, Gross National Happiness and the Sustainable Development Fee.
4. **Map**: 23 clickable places across the country, filterable by type.
5. **Peaks**: an elevation chart of the unclimbed Himalaya, with Everest for scale.
6. **Skies**: prayer flags you can hold the wind for, with the Thunder Dragon in the clouds.
7. **Seasons**: a month-by-month festival calendar with a countdown to the next fixed-date festival.
8. **Experiences** and **Plan**: three itineraries plus a fee calculator (SDF and visa).

Every scene is painted procedurally on `<canvas>`, so no photographs or image hosting are needed. The only external script is d3 (cdnjs); map outlines come from Natural Earth via `world-atlas` and are inlined.

Facts were checked in October 2026. SDF is USD 100 per night until 31 August 2027. Festival dates follow the lunar calendar; confirm before booking.
